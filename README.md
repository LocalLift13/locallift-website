# LocalLift — Official Website

> **"Your Business Deserves to Be Found."**  
> Affordable Websites, Modern Marketing, and Customer Systems for Local Service Businesses.

---

## 🚀 Overview

**LocalLift** is a complete client acquisition and online storefront website designed to sell websites, local marketing, customer lead tracking, and Google optimization to local businesses (landscapers, cleaners, mobile auto detailers, handymen, small cafes, and repair shops).

### 🎯 Key Accomplishments
1. **Convinces Local Business Owners:** Highlights the real pain points ("The Struggle is Real..." — missing calls, trapped on Facebook, no time for marketing) vs the solution ("With LocalLift...").
2. **Shows What You Can Do:** Interactive demonstration websites for 6 core local trades with desktop/mobile view toggles and live interactive features.
3. **Makes Buying Simple & Frictionless:** 3 transparent pricing packages ($399 Starter, $599 Business Growth, $799+ Full-Service) plus an interactive proposal builder.
