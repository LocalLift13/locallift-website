Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Do domain and hosting fees cost extra?

A domain name is typically ~$12 to $15 per year paid directly to standard registrars like Namecheap or Google Domains so you retain 100% legal ownership. We handle the technical linking for free. Hosting is included free for your first 30 days, after which you can use our managed $49/mo plan or host it on your own server.

What are the payment terms?

We work on a straightforward 50/50 split. 50% deposit to commence work and secure your project schedule, and the remaining 50% only after you review, test, and approve the completed website before it is launched live.

What if I need changes after the site is launched?

All packages include dedicated revision rounds before launch. After launch, clients on our Care Plan ($49/mo) receive ongoing photo, phone number, and pricing updates whenever needed. Clients without a care plan can request updates on an hourly basis ($65/hr).
